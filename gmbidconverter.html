What is a Place ID?

The Place ID is Google's unique identifier for locations in Google Maps. It starts with "ChIJ" and is used in the Google Maps API.

Use it for embedding maps, linking to specific locations, or integrating with Google's location services.

What is a CID?

The CID (Customer ID) is Google's internal numeric identifier for business profiles. It's a long number unique to each location.

Use it for direct Google Maps links, review management tools, or tracking specific business locations.

How to Use This Tool
